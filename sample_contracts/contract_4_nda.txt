NON-DISCLOSURE AND NON-COMPETE AGREEMENT

1. CONFIDENTIALITY: The Employee shall not disclose any trade secrets or proprietary information of the Company to any third party during or after employment.

2. NON-COMPETE: For a period of 24 months following termination of employment, the Employee shall not work for any competitor of the Company anywhere in the world.

3. NON-SOLICITATION: The Employee shall not solicit any clients or employees of the Company for a period of 12 months after leaving the Company.

4. INJUNCTIVE RELIEF: The Employee acknowledges that a breach of this agreement will cause irreparable harm, and the Company shall be entitled to seek an injunction without posting a bond.

5. GOVERNING LAW: This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and all disputes shall be settled by binding arbitration in Wilmington, DE.
