RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. RENT: The monthly rent for the premises is $1,200, payable on the 1st of each month. A late fee of $100 applies if rent is not received by the 5th.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT: A security deposit of $1,200 is required. The Landlord may withhold any portion of the deposit for "general cleaning" at their sole discretion upon move-out.

3. REPAIRS: The Tenant is responsible for all minor repairs under $100. For larger repairs, the Tenant must notify the Landlord, who will respond within a "reasonable timeframe" at their convenience.

4. GUESTS: No guests are permitted to stay overnight for more than 2 consecutive nights without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

5. RIGHT OF ENTRY: The Landlord reserves the right to enter the premises at any time without notice to conduct inspections or show the property to prospective tenants.
