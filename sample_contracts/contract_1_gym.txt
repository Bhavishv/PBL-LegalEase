GYM MEMBERSHIP AGREEMENT

1. MEMBERSHIP TERM: The membership begins on the date of signing and shall continue for a period of 12 months.

2. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL: This agreement shall automatically renew for successive 12-month terms at the then-current rate unless the Member provides written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days prior to the expiration of the current term.

3. MONTHLY FEES: The Member agrees to pay a monthly fee of $50. Payments are processed automatically via credit card on the 1st of each month.

4. CANCELLATION FEE: If the Member terminates this agreement before the end of the initial 12-month term, a cancellation fee equal to 100% of the remaining balance of the contract shall be immediately due and payable.

5. LIABILITY WAIVER: The Member agrees to waive all rights to sue the Gym for any injuries sustained on the premises, regardless of whether such injuries were caused by the Gym's negligence.
