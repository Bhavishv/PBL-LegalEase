FREELANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. SERVICES: The Freelancer agrees to provide web development services as described in the attached Statement of Work.

2. COMPENSATION: The Client shall pay the Freelancer $2,000 upon completion of the project. Payment is due within 15 days of invoice receipt.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY: Both parties agree to keep all proprietary information shared during the project confidential.
